# Regression

This course focused on the theory and application of regression methods for analyzing real-world data and addressing research questions. Emphasis was placed on 
model development, statistical inference, diagnostics, prediction, interpretation, and critical evaluation of regression analyses.

## Key Skills and Learning Outcomes

* Applied **probability, random variables, statistical distributions, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing** within the context of regression analysis.
* Identified research questions and real-world datasets where **regression methods** can provide meaningful statistical insight.
* Developed graphical displays and visualizations to explore relationships among variables and assess regression data.
* Formulated appropriate **regression models** to address research questions and evaluate relationships between response and explanatory variables.
* Developed a strong understanding of **Least Squares Estimation**, including the derivation of least-squares estimators for simple linear regression.
* Applied statistical inference for regression models, including estimation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and interpretation of statistical output.
* Used statistical software to perform **regression modeling, variable selection, estimation, model diagnostics, and prediction**.
* Interpreted and communicated regression results within the context of the underlying research problem.
* Evaluated **model assumptions, limitations, potential weaknesses, and sources of uncertainty** in regression analyses.
* Represented linear regression models using **matrix notation** and developed an understanding of the mathematical foundations underlying regression estimation.
* Explored modern statistical methods that extend or build upon the **linear regression framework**.
* Designed and conducted a substantial project using **real-world data and regression analysis** to investigate a research problem from formulation through interpretation.
* Critically evaluated the **strengths and limitations of regression and quantitative methods**, including the appropriate use of statistical techniques in applied research.
